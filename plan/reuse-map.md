# Reuse map: what to take from Basalt, and what to cut

Everything here was read from `/Users/wayne/code/basalt` at `664a963`. Line numbers are from that checkout. Classes: **A** reuse unchanged · **B** reuse with the listed change · **C** encryption or pairing specific, delete · **D** tests.

**Corrected totals.** The round numbers below were checked and did not hold. Measured at `664a963`: Go 13,588 non-test in 18 files and 22,895 test in 73 files (536 top-level `Test…` functions, not "about 600"); TypeScript core 20,802 non-test in 36 files and 21,301 test in 56 files; plugin 15 source and 21 test files; TypeScript MCP 5,246 non-test in 21 files. Individual large-file lengths in the tables below are accurate.

**Do not read a reuse percentage out of this file.** The packages classed A are about 2,929 of 13,588 Go lines, and PLAN.md's "roughly 80 percent reusable unchanged" was never measured. More importantly, textual reuse inside a modified file is not retained assurance when authentication, entry validation, body representation, and commit behaviour all change. PLAN §2.1 replaces the percentage with a per-symbol ledger: copied behaviour, changed contract, the test carrying the guarantee, and the evidence after the change.

**Two line references in this file are wrong** and are corrected in place below: `commit`/`commitMany` (the cited range is the tail of one function; the definitions are `commitMany` at `session.go:1608` and `commit` at `:1968`; an earlier revision of this note had them swapped) and `chunks.Store.Size` (the signature is `Size(vaultID, name) (int64, bool)` at `chunks.go:331`, not `Size(name)`). Assume others may be off by a few lines and confirm before editing.
